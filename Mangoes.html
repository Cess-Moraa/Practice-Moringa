All about mangoes

Eating mangoes

Mangoes are delicious! Here are some ways to enjoy them:

  • By themselves
  • Smoothies
  • Fruit salad
  • With coconut rice

Mangoes are fruits that have a range of possible health benefits. The nutrients they contain may help boost eye, skin, and hair health and prevent cancer and heart disease. There are many different kinds of mango. The mango is a tropical stone fruit and member of the drupe family. This is a type of plant food with a fleshy outer section that surrounds a shell, or pit. This pit contains a seed. Other members of the drupe family include olives, dates, and coconuts.
